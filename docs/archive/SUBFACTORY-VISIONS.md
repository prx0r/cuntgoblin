# Sub-Factory Visions

*Each sub-factory has its own vision and theme*

---

## Vision Selection Criteria

Each vision must be:
1. **Distinct** — Different from other sub-factories
2. **Viable** — Can generate revenue
3. **Scalable** — Can grow beyond MVP
4. **Complementary** — Adds to the portfolio

---

## Selection Process

For each potential vision:
1. **Score against rubric** (novelty, research, feasibility, etc.)
2. **Check market size** (TAM, growth rate)
3. **Check competition** (existing players)
4. **Check defensibility** (moat potential)
5. **Check complementarity** (adds to portfolio?)

If score ≥7 and market ≥$1B, add as sub-factory.
