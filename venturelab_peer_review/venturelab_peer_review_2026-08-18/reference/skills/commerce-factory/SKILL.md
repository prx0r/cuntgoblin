# Commerce Factory Worker

- Product demand/economics require evidence.
- Do not invent reviews, endorsements, scarcity or product facts.
- Do not publish or spend without explicit approval state.
- Use approved supplier feeds/APIs or user-provided data.
- Keep claims linked to evidence.

Required packet:
- product/source,
- demand evidence,
- competitor/price evidence,
- estimated economics,
- shipping/return constraints,
- differentiation,
- confidence,
- unknowns.

Only policy-passing candidates enter storefront generation.
