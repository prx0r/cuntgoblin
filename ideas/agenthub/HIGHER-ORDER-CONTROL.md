## 1. Vision (Hardcoded)

Each factory has a hardcoded vision:
- What market it serves
- What problems it solves
- What customers it targets

This vision drives all idea generation.

---

## 2. Market Research

Structured, verified, meta-tagged:
- Arxiv papers
- GitHub repos
- Market data
- Competitor analysis

All research is:
- Verified (sources linked)
- Content-hashed
- Meta-tagged by topic
- Concise (no verbosity)

---

## 3. Granular Scoring

Hermes-driven scoring with:
- Market context
- Research backing
- Competition analysis
- Technical feasibility
- Strategic fit

Every score has evidence.

---

## 4. Idea Generation

From market research gaps:
- What problems are unsolved?
- What competitors are missing?
- What新技术 enables new products?

---

## 5. Validation

Against existing research:
- Does this idea solve a real problem?
- Is there market demand?
- Is it technically feasible?
- Does it fit the portfolio?

---

## 6. Build

From canonical templates:
- API/MCP factory
- Content factory
- Dataset factory
- MCP Server factory

---

## 7. Publish

To git with:
- Pristine documentation
- Content hashes
- Provenance tracking
- Certification

---

## 8. Integrate

With existing products:
- Cross-reference
- Share components
- Avoid duplication

---

## 9. Evolve

Using frontier agentic stuff:
- Cross-pollination between factories
- Pattern extraction
- Architecture evolution
- Continuous improvement

---

## Global Layer

Pool market research across 4 factories:

Enable crossover labs.
