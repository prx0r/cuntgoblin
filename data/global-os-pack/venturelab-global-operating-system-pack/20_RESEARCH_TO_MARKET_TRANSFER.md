# Research → Market Transfer

After reproducing a mechanism, ask:

- What measurable problem does it reduce?
- Which industries/products experience that problem?
- Does it change cost/latency/reliability enough to matter?
- Is there a packaging gap: paper code versus usable API/MCP/library?
- Does Market Intelligence observe real demand?
- Is research velocity high while implementation adoption remains low?

Pattern:

```text
verified research mechanism
+ market pain
+ low implementation adoption
+ feasible packaging
= research-transfer Opportunity
```

The Market Intelligence evidence graph must support the market side.
The reproduction artifact supports the technical side.
