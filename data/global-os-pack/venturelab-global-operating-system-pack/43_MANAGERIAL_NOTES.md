# Managerial Notes

## Throughput != progress
Measure certified/useful outcomes, evidence gain and cost per success—not number of agent calls.

## Verification is capacity
Reserve workers/budget for tests, independent review and clean-room release.

## Products impose maintenance liabilities
Portfolio scores subtract ongoing source/dependency/deployment burden.

## Reuse before spawn
New factory/oracle/architecture/service requires a real fit gap.

## Schedule frequency is economic
Poll frequency should reflect:
`value_of_change × probability_of_change / polling_cost`.

## Stop and retire cleanly
Preserve outcomes/evidence from failed products and factories; do not delete history.
