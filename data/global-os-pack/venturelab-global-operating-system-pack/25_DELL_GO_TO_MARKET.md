```text
/models/<slug>
/providers/<slug>
/deals/<slug>
/free/<capability>
/compare/<a>-vs-<b>
```

Every page should show:
- as-of date
- evidence/method
- useful unique facts
- API/MCP counterpart

Do not produce thin paraphrased SEO pages.
