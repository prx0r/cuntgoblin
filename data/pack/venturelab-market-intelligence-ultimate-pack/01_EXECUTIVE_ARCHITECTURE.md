## What is permanent

Keep stable:

- object identity
- original artifacts
- observation provenance
- timestamps
- source-family identity
- immutable run records
- score method versions
- decision records
- release/outcome history

Everything else may evolve.

## Market Intelligence is upstream of idea generation

Wrong:

```text
idea → web search → score
```

Correct:

```text
continuous market model
        ↓
structural opportunity
        ↓
many possible solution hypotheses
        ↓
product/factory resolution
```

Research is amortized across every future idea.

## Factory != product archetype

A factory defines WHAT economic/technical domain it searches.

Example:

`Agent Infrastructure Factory`

may output:
- API/MCP
- benchmark
- agent system
- registry
- library

A product archetype defines HOW a chosen solution is compiled and certified.

This distinction must exist in schemas.
