## Task state machine

```text
OBSERVE
→ DERIVE
→ AUDIT
→ DECIDE
```

Do not let the same context both produce and certify a high-impact Opportunity.

## Roles

- Scout
- Normalizer
- Signal Analyst
- Opportunity Miner
- Market Auditor
- Solution Architect
- Factory Resolver
- Factory Genesis Architect
- Product Builder
- Certifier
- Outcome Analyst
- Evolution Controller

Models may be shared; contexts and permissions should not be.
